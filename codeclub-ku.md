The Pharma Student Coding Club at UCPH brings together students who want to become more code literate — in a relaxed setting with coffee, cake, and good company.

## Spring 2026 Learning Objectives

By the end of the semester, you will be able to:

1. **Master essential programming vocabulary** — understand the language developers speak
2. **Read, write, and understand simple code** — move from passive to active engagement
3. **Identify common errors and solve them** — build confidence through debugging
4. **Investigate and decipher complex code** — develop strategies for reading unfamiliar code

## What We Offer

- Coffee, cake, and a warm place to study
- Mentoring in programming and data science
- Hands-on, self-paced practice sessions
- A community of like-minded students

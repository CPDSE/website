      # Affiliation for Industry and Institutional Leaders

      The CPDSE Fellow Program offers an individual affiliation model for experienced professionals from biotech, pharma, digital health, and regulatory bodies who want to engage meaningfully with pharmaceutical data science education and research.

      Fellows are a flexible alternative to a traditional advisory board — joining as individuals rather than through their organisation. They contribute expertise and perspective to the center, and in return gain strategic access to talent, research, and the next generation of pharmaceutical data scientists.

      Fellows act as ambassadors for data science in the pharmaceutical field, bridging academic knowledge and industry practice.

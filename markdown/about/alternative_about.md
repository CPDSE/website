      We put people first — not as end-users of a system, but as the ones who ask the questions, build the methods, and carry understanding forward. Data is the medium through which modern drug development thinks. And drugs are what make the work matter: at the end of every dataset, every model, every curriculum decision, there is a patient.

      Artificial intelligence is accelerating this transformation in ways no curriculum can fully anticipate. Teaching AI literacy matters — but literacy without judgment is fragile. What we are building is the domain-grounded understanding that makes it possible to work with AI well: to know what a model is actually doing with pharmacokinetic data, to recognise where it fails, and to take responsibility for the decisions it informs.

      This is what CPDSE is built around. Not a syllabus. Not a competency framework. A conviction that pharmaceutical data science is a discipline still finding its shape — and that the people who will shape it need more than training. They need a community where that work can happen.

  - color: soft-white
    title: "How we work"
    intro: "CPDSE spans UCPH and SDU. Our members include researchers, educators, students, and industry fellows — with different roles, different time commitments, and different entry points into the work."
    content: |
      What holds us together is not an organisational chart. It is a shared conviction that good work requires transparency, iteration, and the willingness to let purpose evolve as understanding deepens.

      We make decisions by consent. We work in time-boxed cycles. We share information openly by default. And we try to build things that are good enough to try, rather than waiting for things that are too perfect to risk.

      These are not management principles. They are expressions of how we believe knowledge actually develops — in dialogue, through iteration, with honest disagreement along the way.

  - color: soft-white

    title: Accelerate your development
    content: |
      The CPDSE scholarships are designed to accelerate a student's development, support academic exploration, strengthen their CV, and prepare them for research-related careers in pharmaceutical data science.

      The scholarships are intended for MSc students as well as BSc students in their final year of studies. The scholarship amounts to **DKK 10,000 per month for 5–6 months**. It is expected that the student does not take on paid work during the scholarship or receive other grants.

  - color: forest-green
    eyebrow: Eligibility
    title: Student and supervisor affiliation
    content: |
      The scholarships are open to students enrolled in one of the following CPDSE core programs either at SDU or UCPH:

      - BSc and MSc in Pharmacy
      - MSc in Pharmaceutical Sciences
      - BSc and MSc in Medicinal Chemistry

      Furthermore, the scholarships are open to other SDU- or UCPH-students with affiliation with CPDSE.

      At least one of the supervisors must be affiliated with CPDSE and based at one of the seven contributing departments at SDU or UCPH (for details see [Researchers & Units](/researchers-units/)). The CPDSE-affiliated supervisor is expected to relate to CPDSE and contribute to the CPDSE community.

  - color: soft-white
    columns:
      - content: |
          ## Student engagement
          Scholarship holders are expected to advance and contribute to the CPDSE student community. Scholarship holders are invited to participate and engage in CPDSE scientific and social activities, including the CPDSE retreat, and are expected to present their project and work at events organized by CPDSE or by the CPDSE core programs.

      - content: |
          ## Outputs and publication
          The output of the scholarship (a report, a 'digital product', an app, a database, a pharmaceutical data science teaching element, code etc.) should be openly accessible, in adherence with open science practices and disseminated in an appropriate way; such as via the CPDSE home page and/or as a poster in one of the PharmaDataHubs.

          If the output of the scholarship results in a research publication, the Center for Pharmaceutical Data Science Education should be acknowledged for financial support.

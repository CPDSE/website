          ### About the course

          A one-week introduction to computational and AI-driven drug design at the University of Copenhagen, directed by [Professor David Gloriam](https://researchprofiles.ku.dk/en/persons/david-e-gloriam/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}. Each day covers one topic: databases, structure-based drug design, protein and peptide design, data visualisation, and machine learning. Mornings are lectures; afternoons are hands-on workshops.

          Teachers are University of Copenhagen researchers and drug discovery specialists from industry, this year including [Lundbeck](https://www.lundbeck.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [Novo Nordisk](https://www.novonordisk.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, [Gubra](https://www.gubra.dk/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [ZS Discovery](https://www.zs.com/solutions/life-sciences-randd-and-medical/zs-discovery){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}.

          - **Where:** Universitetsparken 2, 2100 Copenhagen Ø
          - **Who:** pharma and biotech professionals with a background in pharmacy, chemistry, biology, medicine or similar. PhD enrolment has closed.
          - **Fee:** DKK 8,040 for industry participants

          - **Apply:** email [david.gloriam@sund.ku.dk](mailto:david.gloriam@sund.ku.dk?subject=Application%3A%20AI-%20%26%20data-driven%20drug%20design%20course%2C%209%E2%80%9313%20November%202026&body=Dear%20David%20Gloriam%2C%0A%0AI%20would%20like%20to%20apply%20for%20a%20place%20on%20the%20AI-%20%26%20Data-Driven%20Drug%20Design%20course%20%289%E2%80%9313%20November%202026%29%20as%20an%20industry%20participant.%0A%0AName%3A%0AOrganisation%3A%0APosition%3A%0AEmail%3A%0A%0AKind%20regards%2C%0A) by Friday 30 October 2026

    eyebrow: Learning objectives
    title: "What you will be able to do"
    columns:
      - content: |
          - Find drug, target and disease information in the right online databases and tools
          - Use structure-based drug design to formulate testable hypotheses for optimising small-molecule leads
          - Apply state-of-the-art protein modelling and peptide design methods such as AlphaFold and RoseTTA
      - content: |
          - Analyse and visualise complex datasets
          - Use machine learning for small-molecule and peptide design, and to find the most important features in a drug or target dataset
          - Leave with a taste of each area, a toolbox for your own projects, and a shared language with computational colleagues

    summary: |
      - **Mon 9 Nov — Databases.** Drug, target and disease databases, an industry talk, and a workshop hunting data for your own project.
      - **Tue 10 Nov — Structure-based drug design.** The docking toolbox, drug–target interactions, hit-to-lead optimisation from [Lundbeck](https://www.lundbeck.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, and a ligand-optimisation competition.
      - **Wed 11 Nov — AI/ML tools for peptides and proteins.** AlphaFold, Rosetta, ProteinMPNN and BindCraft, case stories from [Novo Nordisk](https://www.novonordisk.com/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"} and [Gubra](https://www.gubra.dk/){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, and a binder-design workshop.
      - **Thu 12 Nov — Data visualisation.** Visualisation theory, scripting tools, and a workshop using the AI assistant Claude.
      - **Fri 13 Nov — Data analysis using AI/ML.** Data preparation, predictive machine learning, an industrial case story from [ZS Discovery](https://www.zs.com/solutions/life-sciences-randd-and-medical/zs-discovery){:target="_blank" rel="noopener"}, and a workshop using Claude.

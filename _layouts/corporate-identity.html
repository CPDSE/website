Font

Verdana is widely available, screen-legible at every size, and quietly serious — exactly the qualities a research center asks of its typography. Use it for every body of running text, headline, table, and caption. No mixing in display faces, no decorative substitutes.

Verdana

Colors

The palette splits into three groups: a green family carrying the scientific identity, a gold family adding warmth and approachability, and two neutrals for text and surface. Hover any swatch to copy its hex.

Greens — primary identity 3 colours · growth · knowledge · depth
Primary dark
Forest Green #3C5E3E Original brand green. Used for headers, the navigation band, and dark page sections.
Primary soft
Sage Green #5F7D61 Softer, lighter version of Forest. Pairs with Warm Sand for the second-tier surface.
Primary light
Mint Gray #A9BBAA For backgrounds and subtle accents. Use for tags, dividers, and quiet panels.
Golds — warmth & accent 3 colours · openness · optimism · human touch
Accent deep
Antique Gold #D6C17C Original gold. The default accent — buttons, key links, highlight glyphs.
Accent soft
Warm Sand #E4D7A1 Softer version of Antique Gold, usable for highlights and large coloured surfaces.
Accent light
Ivory Gold Tint #F6F1DC Very light version for backgrounds — section bands, card fills, the Code-Club hero.
Neutrals — text & surface 2 colours · contrast · breathing room
Neutral dark
Charcoal Gray #333333 For body text and high-contrast UI. Use over Soft White and Ivory Gold Tint.
Neutral light
Soft White #F9F9F9 Main page background. Subtly warm — never pure #FFFFFF.

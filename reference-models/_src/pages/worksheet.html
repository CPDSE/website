Worksheet: place your own course or workshop

Work through these prompts on paper or in a shared document. They follow the same order as the examples.

  1. Anchor

    Which substep, or at most two, does the course prepare people for? Browse [[model:where|the value chain]] and note the primary data-science question.

  2. Assess and target

    Pick two or three competencies in [[model:what|the competence model]]. For each, write today's typical level and a realistic target. [[page:what/levels/|The level rubric]] helps you decide.

  3. Deliver

    Sketch one session as a 4Cs sequence, one block for each C: how people connect, a short concept input, hands-on practice, and how they conclude. Check each block against [[page:how/#six-trumps|the Six Trumps]].

CPDSE-teachers use the three models in two main kinds of work. These examples show one of each: a real course, and an imagined workshop for the teachers of a real course.

Curriculum courses
Courses in the pharmacy and pharmaceutical-science programmes at SDU and UCPH that CPDSE helps upgrade or create, so students meet modern pharmaceutical data science.
People Development
Learning for people already in post: researchers, educators, and technical or administrative staff. It includes the FrAIday Bar, a one-hour hands-on session every second Friday, the informal Vibe Café straight after, and tailored workshops built around a group's own work.

Both courses are real and link to their official descriptions. The workshop, starting levels, targets and session designs are illustrative, not assessments of real students or staff.

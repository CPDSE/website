Here are two very different pieces of work: a real bachelor course at SDU, and an imagined People Development workshop for the teachers of a real master's course at UCPH. Both follow the same order (anchor, assess and target, deliver) and each part of that order uses one model.

Both courses are real. The workshop, the starting levels, the targets and the session designs are illustrative, written to show the method.

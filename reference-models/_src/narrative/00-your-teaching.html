Before looking at any model, think of one course or workshop you teach, or would like to teach. Keep it in mind as you read.

  1. Where

    Which part of drug development does it prepare people for?

  2. What

    What should people be able to do afterwards, and how well?

  3. How

    How will they practise it, rather than only hear about it?

Those three questions are the three models. The rest of this page shows how they fit together.

This page is itself planned with the teaching model it describes. The small labels above each part (Connections, Concepts, Concrete Practice, Conclusions) are Sharon Bowman's 4Cs, explained under [[model:how|How]].

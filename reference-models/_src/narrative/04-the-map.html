Put the first two models side by side and you get a grid: the $n_substeps value-chain substeps run across and the $n_domains competence domains run down.

The grid has no fixed links. CPDSE does not keep a list of competencies for each substep. A mark appears only where a course or workshop sets a target, and the four marks here come from the two illustrative examples. The 4Cs are how the teaching is planned to reach each target.

What a CPDSE-teacher does next

  • Anchor your next course or workshop: find its substep in [[model:where|the value chain]].
  • Assess and target: choose two or three competencies in [[model:what|the competence model]] and read [[page:what/levels/|what each level means]].
  • Deliver: plan each session as a 4Cs sequence and check its blocks against [[model:how|the Six Trumps]].

We are pleased to announce that five projects have been selected for funding in the CPDSE PhD project call. Congratulations to all awarded principal investigators — we look forward to seeing these exciting projects develop!

**The awarded projects are:**

- AI to strengthen transparency and reproducibility in real-world evidence (RWE) generation
- LLM-assisted patient–pharmacist conversation to improve communication education
- A Neurosymbolic Framework for Literature-Driven GPCR Interaction Modeling
- From mechanism to prediction: forecasting ligand-specific outcomes across GPCR variants with MD-derived biophysical descriptors
- Modeling release and redistribution of hydrophobic drugs from lipid nanocarriers

These five projects represent a strong and diverse research foundation. We expect the PhD students involved to play an important role in the collaborative research activities emerging from CPDSE, including student-driven science exchange initiatives where participants can shape the format and direction of the work with center support.

Congratulations again to all awardees — we look forward to the next steps!

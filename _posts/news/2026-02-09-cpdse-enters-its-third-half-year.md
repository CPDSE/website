The Center for Pharmaceutical Data Science Education (CPDSE) is entering its third half-year of operation, and with it, a deliberate shift in focus. Having built our internal foundation, we are now turning outward: toward the faculty, students, departments, and industry partners we exist to serve.

Our guiding theme is **"Systematize, Service, Expose."** More than 25 contributors across the University of Copenhagen and the University of Southern Denmark have already committed to concrete deliverables.

**Systematize: Building the Foundation for Scalability**
We are investing in the governance and technical infrastructure needed to make data science a durable part of pharmaceutical education. This includes standardized, one-click coding environments in R and Python, a shared code culture via GitHub, and a refreshed website, ensuring our work is reusable, scalable, and consistently communicated.

**Service: Supporting Faculty and Students**
CPDSE exists to strengthen education in our partner departments. We are collaborating with course teams to integrate data science into existing curricula, launching drop-in support sessions and student code clubs, and offering workshops and bootcamps to equip staff with relevant data science skills.

**Expose: Bridging Academia and Industry**
We are connecting our students and research with the broader pharmaceutical world through industry-linked projects, data science exchange events, and a methodological consulting framework. We are also growing our network of affiliated faculty and increasing our presence in external forums.

As we build more robust systems, we remain committed to a founding principle: *safe enough to try.* The pursuit of the perfect infrastructure should never delay the services our community needs today.

## Background

The CPDSE website communicates the centre well, but it could do more to show the real people and personality behind the work. This project is about making the site — and our identity more broadly — feel more human and authentic.

## What you'll do

- Plan and coordinate photography of CPDSE people and activities
- Propose ways to weave more authentic voices and stories into the site
- Apply changes consistently with the existing visual identity guidelines
- Suggest where the identity itself could evolve to feel more genuine

## Requirements

Interest in design, communications, or photography, and an eye for visual storytelling. Familiarity with our [Visual Identity](/visual-identity/) guidelines is a plus.

## Background

Pharmacy school educational material spans many courses and formats, but there's no shared structure connecting concepts across the curriculum. A knowledge graph could make those connections visible and searchable.

## What you'll do

- Collect curriculum and course material across the pharmacy programme
- Parse the material and extract topics, concepts, and relationships
- Build and visualise the result as a knowledge graph
- Suggest analyses and research questions the graph makes possible

## Requirements

Interest in data engineering and/or NLP. Comfort with graph databases is a plus, but willingness to learn is enough. Interest in educational content is helpful.

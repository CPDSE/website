## Background

Are you a curious mind ready to combine pharmaceutical knowledge with data science to make a real impact? Lundbeck is opening the door to its Solid Bulk Production for MSc thesis students who want to work where data meets manufacturing at scale.

You'll get access to years of historical process data, live production streams, and dedicated sensor data — a rare playground for the right student. You will explore how raw material variation is connected to the final product characteristics. In an operation where every percent of optimization matters, your analyses can translate directly into better processes, less waste, and more medicine reaching patients.

## Perspective

This is not an MSc thesis that ends on a shelf. You'll work alongside experienced process and data specialists and have the genuine opportunity to see your ideas implemented in a live production environment.

Bring your pharmaceutical background, your curiosity for data sciences, and let's optimize something that matters — together.

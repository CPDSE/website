## Background

Pharma companies are launching AI initiatives at very different paces and levels of maturity. A systematic view of how organisations are actually approaching this transformation — what's working, what isn't — would be valuable to the whole field.

## What you'll do

- Investigate how a range of pharma organisations are approaching AI transformation
- Map out strategies, structures, and progress across cases
- Codify recurring patterns into a framework others can use
- Communicate the findings clearly, e.g. as a report or visual model

## Requirements

Interest in strategy and organisational change, comfort conducting interviews or qualitative research, and interest in the pharma/AI intersection.

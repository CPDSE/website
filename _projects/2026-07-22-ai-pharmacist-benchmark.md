## Background

Large language models keep getting better at professional exams in medicine, law, and other fields — but how do they actually perform on the material a pharmacist needs to know? This project sets out to systematically test that question.

## What you'll do

- Compile a benchmark set of pharmacy exam questions and coursework material
- Evaluate a range of AI models against the benchmark
- Analyse where models succeed and where they fail, and why
- Compare AI performance to what's expected of human pharmacy students
- Discuss the implications for pharmacy education and the future role of AI in pharmacy practice

## Requirements

Interest in AI/NLP and comfort working with LLM APIs. A pharmacy or pharmacology background is a plus but not required.

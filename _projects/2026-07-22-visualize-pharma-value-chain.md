## Background

It's hard to see at a glance who does what across the pharma value chain, from early research through to patients — and where CPDSE's research groups, partner companies, and courses fit into that picture.

## What you'll do

- Map relevant research groups, companies, and courses onto the pharma value chain
- Design a clear visual representation of the map
- Produce both a physical mural and an interactive online version
- Iterate on the design with input from CPDSE members

## Requirements

Interest in information design or data visualization, comfort with a design tool, and some understanding of the pharma value chain is helpful.

## Background

People come into CPDSE's data science training with very different backgrounds and skill levels, but there's no easy way today to work out where someone actually stands or what they should focus on next.

## What you'll do

- Define a competency model covering the key data science skill areas
- Design test items that reliably assess each competency
- Build a small tool to administer the assessment and score results
- Turn results into concrete, personalised next-step recommendations
- Pilot the suite with real users and iterate on the design

## Requirements

Interest in data science education. Comfort with basic web/app development, or background in assessment/psychometrics, is a plus.
